GOAL: CertWeaver enterprise production readiness — 100/100

Bring the named CertWeaver release and approved production deployment profile
to 100/100 under version 1.0.0 of docs/enterprise-production-readiness.md and
docs/readiness/enterprise-controls.v1.json. Demonstrate all 100 controls at
assured level, all twelve category gates and all activated conditional gates
passing, current release-bound evidence, authenticated independent review,
and the authorized production decision. Preserve the original objective until
it is substantively demonstrated.

REQUIRED INPUTS TO FREEZE AT THE START

- Assessment claim: production launch, existing-service operation, or complete
  product-wide support qualification.
- Product version, final Git commit, artifact digests, framework/catalogue
  version and digest, deployment/profile ID, effective configuration, CI and
  packaging definitions, policy revisions, and target environment.
- Actual staging configuration path and approved access method; secret-system
  or file references without exposing values in chat, Git, logs, or evidence.
- Included modes, adapters and their distinct roles/capabilities, issuers,
  controller/host OS and architecture, supported runtime/version matrix,
  tenants, trust domains, failure domains, and external dependencies.
- Accountable service, platform, PKI, security, recovery, release, operations,
  and independent review authorities.
- Numeric SLOs, scheduling/issuance deadlines, consumer availability and
  renewal margins, revocation/exposure bounds, RTO/RPO, failover limits,
  alert/response targets, workload/quotas/headroom, soak duration, maintenance,
  evidence retention/freshness, and patch/rotation deadlines.
- Approved staging fault-injection and recovery boundaries, stop conditions,
  cleanup/rollback, maintenance window, and permission constraints.

If a required input is missing, ask for the minimum specific information early
and continue independent repository work. Do not invent infrastructure,
credentials, named reviewers, organizational approvals, or service targets.
The default technical scope is production RKE2 HA plus explicitly selected
adapters; do not silently narrow any broader scope the user actually requests.

ASSESSMENT RULES

1. Read the framework, complete catalogue, assessment schema, existing
   production scorecard, validation report, architecture, operations/security
   docs, repository instructions, and current authoritative implementation.
   The existing CLI's fixed 97/100 and ten-control evidence gate use a different
   rubric; do not carry their numerical score into this framework.
2. Copy docs/readiness/enterprise-assessment.template.json into an approved
   restricted working location. Start unassessed and populate facts with
   evidence. Each of the 100 stable controls is worth exactly one point.
3. Use cumulative maturity levels: 0=unknown/failed, 1=defined (0.25 point),
   2=implemented (0.50), 3=verified (0.75), 4=assured (1.00). Award a level only
   when its requirements and all preceding requirements are proved. An
   observed acceptance failure earns zero until fixed and reverified. A
   blocker may retain only valid, demonstrated lower-level credit.
4. Keep the denominator fixed at 100. Do not delete controls, lower weights,
   weaken criteria, relax evidence expiry, alter gates, round upward, assign
   free N/A points, or redefine scope to manufacture success. A genuine profile
   change requires explicit authorized approval and a separately versioned
   100-point profile before assessment; preserve the earlier result.
5. Keep software implementation, executed verification, runtime effectiveness,
   organizational approval, and evidence integrity distinct. Mocks, fixtures,
   configured workflows, example inventories, empty outputs, and typed review
   names cannot substitute for real live proof or authenticated approval.
6. Critical/high findings, exposed keys/credentials, unauthorized issuance,
   broken isolation, unproven required recovery, or an unknown/failed mandatory
   gate block the applicable release. Risk acceptance cannot create a passing
   control or an assured point. Every control is required for 100, including
   controls that are not themselves mandatory release gates.

WORK TO COMPLETE

- Assess all 100 controls against the current checkout and external state.
  Record proven, contradicted, unknown, stale, failed, and blocked assertions;
  exact acceptance results; owner; evidence; and the next remediation action.
- Remediate configuration/automation safety, privileged execution boundaries,
  targeting, prerequisites, idempotence, drift, partial failure, upgrade and
  rollback behavior, portable installed resources, and unsupported/no-op paths.
- Qualify certificate/identity/trust policy, custody, allowed and denied
  requests, actual consumer delivery, renewal/adoption, key rotation,
  invalidation/revocation, CA rollover, and retirement. Use the frozen role and
  capability matrix for complementary adapters; do not demand that trust-manager
  issue certificates or enable independent proxy ACME to fake lifecycle proof.
- Validate effective IAM, tenant isolation, host/workload/network controls,
  secret confidentiality, protected audit trails, abuse handling, and access
  removal. Respect approved host-policy decisions; do not disable protections
  merely to make deployment succeed or bypass a rejected action.
- Verify actual immutable dependency/action references and trusted acquisition,
  clean builds, exact sdist/wheel/image contents, secret exclusion with dirty
  and stale packaging metadata, SBOM/licenses, release identity, least-privilege
  permissions, signed provenance verification, and security release gates.
- Execute meaningful positive, negative, integration, compatibility, and
  release-artifact tests. Enforce the framework's declared test policy and
  critical behavioral cases. Run actual CI/security checks on the exact
  candidate; files that configure a check do not prove execution success.
- Deploy the actual candidate to authorized representative staging and prove
  the selected support matrix. A deployment-specific score must not become a
  claim that all twenty adapters and every advertised mode were tested.
- Exercise approved node/process/webhook/backend/dependency/partition cases,
  maintenance, client-observed recovery, quorum and ownership safety, cleanup,
  and repeated convergence. Use the declared external-LB equivalent path when
  that is the approved topology.
- Retrieve off-host backups and restore onto isolated/replacement hosts with
  out-of-cluster recovery access, matching bootstrap material, CA/key-store
  continuity, protected custody, integrity checks, alternate dependencies, and
  actual RTO/RPO measurements. Starting etcd alone is not a successful restore.
- Prove working telemetry, missing-data detection, lifetime-aware alerts,
  end-to-end service probes, technical notification delivery, real responder
  triage/escalation/handoff, runbooks, credential operations, incident
  exercises, maintenance, and sustained ownership.
- Execute the approved capacity model, degraded-mode load, renewal storms,
  backlog recovery, endurance/growth, quota and headroom checks. Do not apply
  unapproved load to a public CA or production service.
- Implement and adversarially validate the enterprise evaluator required by
  EVD-04 if it does not exist. Recompute totals and gates from trusted source
  records; do not trust editable summary totals. The supplied JSON Schema is
  a structural contract, not a complete evaluator or an authorization system.
- Reuse valid existing evidence only after subject, freshness, and release-delta
  review. Re-run affected failed/invalidated checks; do not repeat unrelated
  passing work without a reason. Retain failures and corrective reruns.

EVIDENCE AND FINAL DELIVERABLES

Produce the completed assessment, scope/objective record, source/artifact
manifest, per-control evidence and verification references, findings/exception
register, change-impact review, approval records, and a self-contained readiness
report. The report must show category scores, coverage/unknowns, every gate,
compatibility scope, expected versus actual service/recovery/capacity results,
limitations, owners, reviewer authority, freshness, and the production decision.
Sanitize shared evidence without destroying its measurement. Protect originals
and verify controlled references, hashes, provenance, identities, timestamps,
and required separation of duties. Another model's review is useful assistance,
not automatic organizational authorization.

DEFINITION OF DONE

All100 catalogue controls are current assured level 4, totaling exactly 100/100;
all twelve category gates and activated conditional gates PASS; the final
immutable release/configuration/environment match the evidence; relevant
CI/security checks and actual staging lifecycle/failure/recovery/performance
qualification passed; no active exception substitutes for an unmet criterion;
and authenticated independent review plus the responsible release authorities'
production decision are recorded. Every explicit acceptance assertion must be
supported by authoritative evidence at the same scope.

If external infrastructure, access, policy approval, targets, or review authority
is unavailable, preserve the honest partial result and identify the exact
blocked control/input/owner. Follow the platform's goal blocked-audit policy.
Do not mark the goal complete until the full definition of done is true and
verified; do not replace it with repository-only readiness or a cosmetic score.
